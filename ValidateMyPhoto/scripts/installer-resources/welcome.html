Welcome to Validate My Photo

Validate My Photo uses multi-layer forensic analysis to determine whether digital images are authentic photographs or AI-generated / AI-manipulated content.

This installer will place Validate My Photo.app in your /Applications folder. All processing is performed locally on your Mac — no images or data are ever sent to external servers.

System Requirements: macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later.
